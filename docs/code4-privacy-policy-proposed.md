## Local route planning

Sugarglider helps you plan outdoor routes. After a supported region is downloaded,
the Android planner calculates routes on your device using installed map, routing,
place and nature data. Normal route planning does not send your route to a
Sugarglider routing or sharing server. GPX inspection also runs locally.

## Private foreground location

With your permission, the planner uses device location to show your private current
position and center or follow the map. If permission was already granted, private
foreground location can resume when you reopen the planner. The location watch
stops when the page is hidden. You can revoke location permission in Android
settings and continue planning manually.

This release does not send live location to a Sugarglider sharing service and does
not run a location-sharing foreground service. It does not require background
location permission. Social sharing, outings and live-location sharing are
postponed and unavailable in this release. There is no configurable sharing server
or Sugarglider social account in this release.

## Regional downloads and hosting

Offline regional files are hosted by GitHub, Inc. using GitHub Releases and GitHub
Pages. Downloading or checking a regional file makes an HTTPS request to that host.
The host receives connection metadata such as your IP address and requested files
under its own privacy practices. Local planning does not require a routing server.
No Sugarglider social or sharing backend is provided for this release.

## Local storage, exports and deletion

Installed regional data and planner preferences remain on your device. Use region
removal controls to remove installed regions. Android app storage controls or
uninstallation remove the corresponding application data.

When you export GPX, you choose the destination using Android's document picker.
The storage provider or app you select controls the exported copy and may sync it
according to its settings. Removing Sugarglider does not delete exported copies.

## Advertising and analytics

The audited release contains no advertising or analytics integration. Static file
hosts may process connection metadata under their own policies. Contact the
publisher above for privacy questions; do not send sensitive location information
unless necessary and requested through an appropriate channel.
